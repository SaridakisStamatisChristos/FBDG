# Historical Research Material

This directory preserves dated research snapshots, earlier paper states, bibliography states, and continuity notes for provenance and reproducibility.

These files are **historical and non-authoritative**. Current theorem status, novelty wording, proof boundaries, reproducibility instructions, and manuscript claims are defined by the active repository materials, especially:

- `../STATUS.md` for current evidence and novelty labels;
- `../paper/fbdg.md` for the canonical manuscript source;
- `../proof/fixed_support_theorem.md` for the focused current proof package;
- `../REPRODUCIBILITY.md` and `../validation/` for executable validation scope;
- `../audit/` for dated research and publication-readiness audits.

Historical files may intentionally retain superseded wording, research-session instructions, rejected branches, earlier proof states, or unresolved obligations. They should not be cited as the current scientific state unless a current authoritative document explicitly incorporates them.
